**1/6** 🧵
Malaysia is one of only 4 countries out of 58 without a national policy on how the government owns its companies. 🇲🇾

I went through the new 152-page OECD report on state-owned enterprises so you don't have to. Here's what I found 👇

#Malaysia

**2/6**
First, the scale is huge 🌍
💡 33,834 large SOEs worldwide
💡 USD 141 trillion in assets, about 126.0% of global GDP
💡 Around 43 million jobs

But they earn less. Median ROE is 4.7% for SOEs vs 6.4% for private firms. 📉

**3/6**
Where Malaysia falls behind 🚩
🔹 No single state ownership policy (only India, Luxembourg and New Zealand share this)
🔹 Public service obligations are paid indirectly, without separate accounts
🔹 SOEs aren't required to disclose their public policy goals

#CorporateGovernance

**4/6**
Where Malaysia does well ✅
🔹 Boards have full authority over company strategy (only 25 of 58 jurisdictions do this)
🔹 Malaysia now produces aggregate reports on its SOE sector

So it's not all bad news 🙂

**5/6**
What could come next? 🧭
1️⃣ A national ownership policy for all SOEs
2️⃣ Cost-based, transparent PSO compensation
3️⃣ A central unit to coordinate ownership

⚠️ This way forward and the Malaysia status labels are my own reading, not official OECD or government views.
